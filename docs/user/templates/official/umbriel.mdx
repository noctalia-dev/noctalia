The built-in Umbriel template generates colors for regular and scratchpad borders,
the insertion hint, fullscreen backdrop, and overview tint.

## Setup

Enable **Umbriel** under **Settings → Templates → Compositors**.

Noctalia writes the rendered template to `~/.config/umbriel/noctalia.toml` and adds
it to the `files` array in the `[include]` section of `config.toml`. Existing include
entries are preserved. Umbriel reloads both files automatically when they change.
